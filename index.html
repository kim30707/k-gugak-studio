The concert

No stage. No microphone. Just the room.

  • GayageumA twelve-string zither plucked with bare fingers. Bright, fluid, and the Korean sound most visitors recognise.
  • GeomungoSix thick strings struck with a bamboo stick. Deep, percussive, and once the instrument of scholars.
  • PansoriKorea's sung storytelling — one voice carrying a whole drama, listed by UNESCO as intangible heritage.
  • MinyoFolk songs everyone in Korea knows. You will be invited to join the refrain.
Play it yourself · Experience B

A Korean instrument you can play in ten minutes.

A real gayageum takes years. So we built our own tablet gugak instruments: touch the strings on the screen and you get the true sound of the instrument, without the years.

After watching how the real instruments are played, each guest gets a tablet. We learn a simple Korean melody step by step, split into parts, and play it as an ensemble. Children, grandparents and people who "can't play anything" all manage it — that is exactly who we designed it for.

FAQ

Questions guests ask

I have never played an instrument. Is Experience B for me?

Yes. The tablet instruments were made for first-timers. We teach everything from the first note, and nobody plays alone.

Can children join?

Children are welcome with an adult. The concert is quiet and close-up, so it suits children who can sit and listen for about thirty minutes.

How do I pay?

Book on this page and pay by bank transfer in Korean won. The account details appear with your booking code and are emailed to you.

What if my plans change?

Tell us as early as you can. Changes are free up to 3 days before; refunds follow the table above.

Is it good for a rainy day?

It is one of the best things to do in Busan when it rains — everything happens indoors, and rain on a tile roof suits the music.
